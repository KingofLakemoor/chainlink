Around the time Southwest Autism Research and Resource Center (SARRC) was founded, the incidence of an autism diagnosis was 1 in 500; today it is 1 in 54.

Seven years ago I took a chance on myself and moved across the country to start on a new career path to work for a company that shared all the same values that I hold. I am participating in YES Day for Autism not only to support the company I am so proud to be a part of but also to raise critical autism funding for many nonprofit autism organizations throughout the state. Importantly, all dollars will be raised and kept in Arizona for Arizona.
YES Day for Autism is as much about saying YES to autism awareness, support, and inclusion as it is to saying YES to statewide collaboration. Every dollar raised will help the state expand from Phoenix as the most autism-friendly city to Arizona as the most autism-friendly state!

This year I am challenging myself and increased my fundraising goal of previous years! Please make a donation or join my team to say YES to making a difference and creating opportunities for children, teens, and adults with autism!
